\# QRate



QRate is a QR-based customer feedback and digital menu platform built with FastAPI.



Businesses can provide customers with a QR code that gives access to restaurant information, a digital menu, customer feedback forms, and contact options.



\## Features



\- QR-based restaurant pages

\- Customer ratings and feedback

\- Google Reviews integration

\- Digital restaurant menu

\- Greek and English support

\- Admin dashboard

\- Superadmin and owner roles

\- Restaurant management

\- Menu category and item management

\- Customer contact requests

\- Email notifications

\- Review and contact management



\## Tech Stack



\- Python

\- FastAPI

\- SQLAlchemy

\- SQLite

\- Jinja2

\- HTML/CSS
